Emulator Structure

MAIN LOOP
For each instruction:
	Look at opcode, to decide how it needs to be handled.
	Call relevant handling function, that then slices into relevant fields of the argument, and then carries out its effect
		^ This should probs be two functions, one to parse, one to emulate
	Repeat until `halt` is reached.

REGISTERS
	General purpose registers in an array?
	Flag registers in an array/struct
 
MEMORY
Undecided, two ideas so far:
	Allocate everything and use pointer arithmetic (very bad for space but will be super fast once its running)
	Hashmap - only end up storing addresses that have been accessed (bit slower at runtime but as efficient as is possible for space.
